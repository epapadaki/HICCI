Welcome to HICCI

Human-In-the-loop Curation of Cellular Interactions

Infer cell–cell communication — ligand–receptor interactions — from single-cell RNA-seq, then refine the predictions with expert human-in-the-loop curation. The biologically credible interactions are kept and the noise removed, with the evidence for every call in view.

Get started How it works

Overview

Computational tools predict many ligand–receptor interactions, and they often disagree and include spurious calls. This platform pairs that inference with a transparent curation step and tools to interpret what remains. A typical analysis has four steps:

  1. Add a dataset. Upload an .h5ad file or load the example, confirm the cell-type (and optional sample/group) columns, and prepare it.
  2. Run a tool. Choose one of CellPhoneDB, NATMI, CellChat, SingleCellSignalR or ICELLNET — or run several and compare them.
  3. Curate. Review each predicted interaction with its computed, pair-level and receptor-level evidence, and keep or reject it. The kept set becomes the curated network.
  4. Interpret. Explore the curated network, its functional enrichment across many libraries, and a literature-grounded draft Discussion of the findings.
